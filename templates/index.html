学费亲民 · 奖学金丰厚Generous Scholarships
中国政府每年为国际学生提供 40,000+ 奖学金名额，覆盖学费、住宿、生活费与医疗保险。40,000+ scholarships per year covering tuition, housing, stipend and medical insurance.
奖学金体系分四层：中国政府奖学金（CSC，本科 ¥2,500/月、硕士 ¥3,000/月、博士 ¥3,500/月）、孔子学院奖学金（面向汉语学习者）、省市奖学金（北京、上海、江苏、广东等多省设立）、校级奖学金（清华、北大等名校均有专属项目）。叠加申请，最高可全额覆盖并结余。
Four-tier scholarship system: Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC, ¥2,500/month for undergraduates, ¥3,000 for master's, ¥3,500 for PhD), Confucius Institute Scholarship, Provincial/Municipal Scholarships (Beijing, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Guangdong), and University Scholarships. Stacked together, they can fully cover costs with surplus.

Weekends for the Great Wall and Forbidden City; holidays for Guilin, Dunhuang and Jiuzhaigou — read China through its landscapes.

FAQ

疑问解答 Frequently Asked Questions

关于来华留学校园生活的常见疑问，一次性为你解答 Common questions about campus life in China, answered at once

+ 留学生宿舍条件怎么样？What are the dormitory conditions for international students?
多数高校为留学生提供专门的留学生公寓，一般为双人间或单人间，配备空调、独立卫浴、热水、书桌衣柜与免费校园 Wi-Fi，部分宿舍楼还设有公共厨房与自习室。宿舍通常位于校园内或步行十分钟以内，安保 24 小时值班，进出需刷卡，安全便利。 Most universities offer dedicated international student dormitories with double or single rooms, air conditioning, en-suite bathroom, hot water, desk and wardrobe, and free campus Wi-Fi. Some buildings have shared kitchens and study lounges. Dorms are on or near campus with 24-hour security and card access.
+ 校园里有哪些餐饮选择？What dining options are available on campus?
大学食堂菜系丰富，涵盖川鲁粤淮扬各大菜系，并设有清真窗口与西餐窗口，价格亲民，一顿正餐约 10-25 元。校园周边通常有小吃街、咖啡馆与便利店。部分留学生公寓设有公共厨房，可自行烹饪家乡风味，饮食适应起来十分方便。 University canteens offer diverse Chinese cuisines — Sichuan, Shandong, Cantonese, Huaiyang — plus halal and Western counters, at affordable prices (10-25 RMB per meal). Snack streets, cafés and convenience stores surround the campus. Some dorms have shared kitchens for cooking your own food.
+ 留学生可以参加哪些社团与文化活动？What clubs and cultural activities can international students join?
每所高校都有上百个学生社团，涵盖书法、太极、武术、茶艺、汉服、民乐、舞蹈、辩论、志愿服务等。学校常组织留学生文化体验活动，如中秋赏月、春节联欢、端午包粽子、京剧脸谱绘制、博物馆参访与短途旅行，是结交中外朋友、融入校园的好机会。 Each university hosts hundreds of student clubs — calligraphy, tai chi, martial arts, tea ceremony, hanfu, folk music, dance, debate and volunteering. Schools regularly organize cultural events for international students: Mid-Autumn moon viewing, Spring Festival galas, making zongzi (sticky-rice dumplings) for the Dragon Boat Festival, Peking opera mask painting, museum visits and short trips — a great way to make friends and integrate into campus life.
+ 校园网络、通讯和快递方便吗？Are internet, communications and delivery services convenient on campus?
校园 Wi-Fi 全覆盖，宿舍可单独办理宽带，月费约 30-80 元。开通中国手机号后可使用微信、支付宝，与中国同学无缝沟通。校园内及门口均设有快递驿站（菜鸟驿站、京东自提柜），淘宝、美团、饿了么等网购与外卖十分发达，日常生活极为便利。 Campus Wi-Fi is fully covered; dorms can subscribe to broadband (30-80 RMB/month). With a Chinese SIM card, WeChat and Alipay make communication with local classmates seamless. Courier stations and self-pickup lockers are on campus, and online shopping and food delivery (Taobao, Meituan, Ele.me) are highly developed, making daily life very convenient.
+ 留学生生病了怎么就医？How do international students get medical care?
留学生入学时统一购买综合医疗保险（约 800 元/年），覆盖门诊、住院与意外伤害。每所高校设有校医院，处理常见病与体检，费用低廉；如需专科治疗可转诊至合作三甲医院。校医院与留学生办公室均可协助就医陪诊，部分医院设有国际部提供英语服务。 International students are covered by comprehensive medical insurance (about 800 RMB/year) including outpatient, inpatient and accident care. Each university has an on-campus hospital for common illnesses and check-ups at low cost; referrals to partner tertiary hospitals are available for specialized care. The campus hospital and international office can assist with visits, and some hospitals have international departments with English service.
+ 节假日可以去哪里体验中国文化？Where can I experience Chinese culture during holidays?
寒暑假与国庆、五一等长假是出行好时机。可游览故宫长城、江南园林、西安兵马俑、桂林山水、云南少数民族村寨；也可参加学校组织的文化考察营，深入乡村、企业、博物馆。高铁网络覆盖全国，学生证购买火车票享有优惠，是探索神州的绝佳方式。 Winter/summer breaks and National Day and May Day holidays are ideal for travel. Visit the Forbidden City and Great Wall, Jiangnan gardens, Xi'an's Terracotta Army, Guilin's landscapes or Yunnan's ethnic villages. Schools also organize cultural study camps to villages, enterprises and museums. China's high-speed rail network covers the whole country, and student ID offers train-ticket discounts — a perfect way to explore China.
