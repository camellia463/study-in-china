SIX REASONS

六大理由 · 心向神州Six Reasons · Heart Set on China

从文脉到奖学金，从文化到前景，六重理由让你笃定来华From heritage to scholarships, from culture to careers — six reasons to choose China

脉

千年文脉 · 世界名校

A Millennium of Heritage · World-Class Universities

从太学书院到现代学府，中国高等教育承千年文脉而启新章。3,000+ 所高校、39 所 985、6 所 QS 百强，囊括清华、北大、复旦等世界名校，学术底蕴与前沿研究并行。

From ancient academies to modern universities, China carries a millennial scholarly lineage into a new era. With 3,000+ institutions, 39 Project 985 universities, and 6 in the QS World Top 100 — Tsinghua, Peking, Fudan and more lead the way. The Double First-Class initiative includes 147 universities and 465 disciplines advancing toward world-class status.

惠

学费亲民 · 奖学金丰厚

Affordable & Generous Scholarships

相比欧美，中国学费与生活成本亲民。中国政府每年提供 40,000+ 全额奖学金名额，覆盖学费、住宿、生活费与医保，更有省市、校级奖学金层层叠加。

Lower tuition and living costs, plus 40,000+ full scholarships each year covering tuition, housing, stipend and insurance, plus provincial and university scholarships layered on top.

融

多元文化 · 安全宜居

Multicultural & Livable

56 个民族、八大方言、南北风物千姿百态。中国城市治安全球领先，深夜出行无忧，移动支付、外卖快递触手可及，留学生活便捷安心。

56 ethnic groups, eight major dialects, diverse scenery north and south, world-leading urban safety, worry-free late-night travel, mobile payment and food delivery at your fingertips — a multicultural, convenient and secure place to live and learn.

途

一带一路 · 广阔前景

Belt and Road Opportunities

一带一路联通 150+ 国家，中资企业出海需要大量懂中文、通中外的人才。在华留学成为进入跨国企业、外交、贸易、基建领域的金色通行证。

The Belt and Road Initiative links 150+ countries. Chinese enterprises going global need talents who know Chinese and bridge cultures. Studying in China is a golden pass to multinational firms, diplomacy, trade and infrastructure.

语

中文魅力 · 全球通用

The Global Reach of Mandarin

中文是全球使用人数最多的语言，HSK 成绩被 1,000+ 海外院校认可。掌握中文，即掌握一把打开 14 亿人口市场与东方智慧的钥匙。

Mandarin — the world's most spoken language, with HSK scores recognized by 1,000+ universities worldwide — unlocks a market of 1.4 billion and the wisdom of the East.

通

高铁通达 · 山河壮丽

High-Speed Rail & Magnificent Landscapes

42,000 公里高铁网纵横南北，北京到上海 4.5 小时，成都到西安 3 小时。周末可游长城故宫，假期可揽桂林山水、敦煌大漠、九寨彩林。

A 42,000 km high-speed rail network spans north and south — 4.5 hours from Beijing to Shanghai, 3 hours from Chengdu to Xi'an. Weekends for the Great Wall and Forbidden City, holidays for Guilin's rivers, Dunhuang's desert and Jiuzhai's forests.

HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

中国高等教育体系China's Higher Education System

从 985 到双一流，三级金字塔筑就学术高地From Project 985 to Double First-Class — a three-tier pyramid of academic excellence

985

985 工程Project 985

1998 年 5 月由教育部启动，遴选 39 所顶尖研究型大学，旨在建设世界一流学府。清华、北大、复旦、上交、浙大、中科大等皆列其中，代表中国高等教育最高水准。

Launched in May 1998 by the Ministry of Education, Project 985 selects 39 top research universities, aiming to build world-class institutions. Tsinghua, Peking, Fudan, SJTU, Zhejiang and USTC are among them, representing the pinnacle of Chinese higher education.

211

211 工程Project 211

1995 年启动，面向 21 世纪重点建设 115 所高等学校和一批重点学科，覆盖各省龙头高校，是 985 之外的国家重点建设序列。

Started in 1995 for the 21st century, Project 211 supports 115 key universities and disciplines, covering leading schools in every province — a national key initiative alongside Project 985.

双
一
流 DFC

双一流建设Double First-Class

2017 年启动的新时代战略，统筹推进世界一流大学与一流学科建设，147 所高校、465 个学科入选。2022 年启动新一轮建设，取代 985/211 成为新时代建设框架。

Launched in 2017, the Double First-Class initiative builds world-class universities and disciplines — 147 universities and 465 disciplines selected. A new round in 2022 replaced 985/211 as the framework for the new era.

SCHOLARSHIP SYSTEM

奖学金体系 · 四重护航Scholarship System · Fourfold Support

从国家到学校，层层覆盖，让留学之路无忧From national to university level, layered coverage for a worry-free journey

国

中国政府奖学金

Chinese Government Scholarship
  • 全额覆盖学费、住宿、生活费、医保Full coverage of tuition, housing, stipend and insurance
  • 本科生 ¥2,500/月 · 硕士 ¥3,000/月 · 博士 ¥3,500/月Undergraduates ¥2,500/month · Master's ¥3,000/month · PhD ¥3,500/month
  • 经中国驻外使领馆或 CSC 网申Apply via Chinese embassies/consulates or CSC online portal
  • 每年 40,000+ 名额，覆盖 180+ 国家40,000+ places annually, covering 180+ countries
孔

孔子学院奖学金

Confucius Institute Scholarship
  • 面向国际中文教师与汉语学习者For international Chinese teachers and Chinese language learners
  • 资助汉语国际教育硕士、汉语本科等Funds Master's in Teaching Chinese to Speakers of Other Languages, Chinese-language undergraduate programs, etc.
  • 需 HSK 成绩，经海外孔子学院推荐Requires HSK scores, recommended by overseas Confucius Institutes
  • 含学费、住宿、生活费与一次性安置费Covers tuition, housing, stipend and a one-time settlement allowance
省

省市奖学金

Provincial & Municipal Scholarship
  • 北京、上海、江苏、广东等多省设立Established by Beijing, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Guangdong and many other provinces
  • 奖励在本地高校就读的优秀留学生Awards outstanding international students at local universities
  • 部分省份全额资助，部分按学历分档Some provinces offer full funding, others tier by degree level
  • 与国家级奖学金可形成补充组合Can be combined with national scholarships as supplementary support
校

校级奖学金

University Scholarship
  • 由各高校自主设立、自主评审Independently established and reviewed by each university
  • 清华、北大、复旦等名校均有专属项目Tsinghua, Peking, Fudan and other top universities have dedicated programs
  • 覆盖全额、半额、新生奖、学业奖Covers full, half, freshman and academic awards
  • 申请窗口与录取通知同步开放Application window opens alongside admission notices

STUDENT LIFE

留学生活 · 衣食住行Student Life · Daily Essentials

从宿舍到餐桌，从地铁到医保，生活无忧方能学有所成From dorms to dining, from transit to insurance — a worry-free life lets you focus on learning

宿

住宿Accommodation

多数高校为留学生提供专门公寓，双人间、单人间可选，月租 ¥400–¥2,000。配备空调、独立卫浴、共用厨房，部分院校可申请校外住宿补贴。 Most universities offer dedicated apartments for international students, with double or single rooms available at ¥400–¥2,000/month. Rooms come with air conditioning, en-suite bathrooms and shared kitchens; some schools offer off-campus housing subsidies.

食

餐饮Dining

校园食堂菜式丰富，一餐 ¥10–¥25 即可吃饱。清真窗口、素食窗口、西餐窗口一应俱全，外卖平台 24 小时配送，南北风味触手可及。 Campus canteens offer a wide variety — a full meal costs ¥10–¥25. Halal, vegetarian and Western counters are all available, and food delivery platforms run 24/7, bringing regional flavors from across China right to your door.

行

交通Transport

地铁、公交、共享单车三网合一，月均通勤支出 ¥200 内。高铁联通全国主要城市，留学生凭学生证可享部分景区与交通优惠。 Subway, bus and bike-share networks are seamlessly connected, with monthly commuting costs under ¥200. High-speed rail links all major cities, and international students can enjoy discounts at select attractions and on transport with a student ID.

医

医保Insurance

留学生需购买综合医疗保险（约 ¥800/年），覆盖门诊、住院、意外伤害。校内设医务室，三甲医院遍布各城，就医便捷。 International students are required to purchase comprehensive medical insurance (about ¥800/year), covering outpatient care, hospitalization and accidental injury. Campus clinics and Grade-A tertiary hospitals across cities make medical care easily accessible.
