HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

中国高等教育体系China's Higher Education System

从 985 到双一流，三级金字塔筑就学术高地From Project 985 to Double First-Class — a three-tier pyramid of academic excellence

985

985 工程Project 985

1998 年 5 月由教育部启动，遴选 39 所顶尖研究型大学，旨在建设世界一流学府。清华、北大、复旦、上交、浙大、中科大等皆列其中，代表中国高等教育最高水准。

Launched in May 1998 by the Ministry of Education, Project 985 selects 39 top research universities, aiming to build world-class institutions. Tsinghua, Peking, Fudan, SJTU, Zhejiang and USTC are among them, representing the pinnacle of Chinese higher education.

211

211 工程Project 211

1995 年启动，面向 21 世纪重点建设 115 所高等学校和一批重点学科，覆盖各省龙头高校，是 985 之外的国家重点建设序列。

Started in 1995 for the 21st century, Project 211 supports 115 key universities and disciplines, covering leading schools in every province — a national key initiative alongside Project 985.

双
一
流 Double First-Class

双一流建设Double First-Class

2017 年启动的新时代战略，统筹推进世界一流大学与一流学科建设，147 所高校、465 个学科入选。2022 年启动新一轮建设，取代 985/211 成为新时代建设框架。

Launched in 2017, the Double First-Class initiative builds world-class universities and disciplines — 147 universities and 465 disciplines selected. A new round in 2022 replaced 985/211 as the framework for the new era.

STUDENT LIFE

留学生活 · 衣食住行Student Life · Daily Essentials

从宿舍到餐桌，从地铁到医保，生活无忧方能学有所成From dorms to dining, from transit to insurance — a worry-free life lets you focus on learning

宿

住宿Accommodation

多数高校为留学生提供专门公寓，双人间、单人间可选，月租 ¥400–¥2,000。配备空调、独立卫浴、共用厨房，部分院校可申请校外住宿补贴。 Most universities offer dedicated apartments for international students, with double or single rooms available at ¥400–¥2,000/month. Rooms come with air conditioning, en-suite bathrooms and shared kitchens; some schools offer off-campus housing subsidies.

食

餐饮Dining

校园食堂菜式丰富，一餐 ¥10–¥25 即可吃饱。清真窗口、素食窗口、西餐窗口一应俱全，外卖平台 24 小时配送，南北风味触手可及。 Campus canteens offer a wide variety — a full meal costs ¥10–¥25. Halal, vegetarian and Western counters are all available, and food delivery platforms run 24/7, bringing regional flavors from across China right to your door.

行

交通Transport

地铁、公交、共享单车三网合一，月均通勤支出 ¥200 内。高铁联通全国主要城市，留学生凭学生证可享部分景区与交通优惠。 Subway, bus and bike-share networks are seamlessly connected, with monthly commuting costs under ¥200. High-speed rail links all major cities, and international students can enjoy discounts at select attractions and on transport with a student ID.

医

医保Insurance

留学生需购买综合医疗保险（约 ¥800/年），覆盖门诊、住院、意外伤害。校内设医务室，三甲医院遍布各城，就医便捷。 International students are required to purchase comprehensive medical insurance (about ¥800/year), covering outpatient care, hospitalization and accidental injury. Campus clinics and Grade-A tertiary hospitals across cities make medical care easily accessible.
